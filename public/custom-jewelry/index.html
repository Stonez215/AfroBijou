Custom jewelry, made for you in Miami

Bring a name, a symbol, a sketch, or a family piece you want remade. Papa Gueye designs it with you and makes it by hand in 18k or 24k gold or sterling silver. Most custom pieces are ready in about 3 days.

How it works

  1. Tell Papa what you see

    A name, a symbol, a sketch, a photo of something you love, or a family piece to remake.

  2. Get your price

    Papa replies with the design approach, the metal and karat, and a price based on that day's gold or silver market.

  3. Made by hand in about 3 days

    Once you approve, he draws the filigree wire, forges, sets stones and polishes the piece himself. Larger work may take longer, and he tells you up front.

What he makes: name pendants and cuffs, Africa and Senegal map pendants, ankh and baobab pendants, filigree rings and earrings, signet and stone rings, and remakes of family jewelry.

Metals: 18k gold, 24k gold, 925 sterling silver, brass and copper.

Custom order questions
Do you make custom gold jewelry?
Yes. Papa makes custom pieces in 18k and 24k gold and in sterling silver: name pieces, pendants, rings, cuffs, and remakes of family jewelry. A custom piece usually takes about 3 days. Larger or detailed work can take longer, and Papa tells you up front.
Can you remake or redesign a family piece?
Yes. Bring the piece or a photo of it. Papa can rework it into a new design or use it as the starting point for something new, and he will explain the options and price before he starts.
Can you make a name necklace or name cuff?
Yes. Name pendants, name cuffs and initial rings are some of Papa's most requested pieces, in 18k or 24k gold or sterling silver.
How are prices set?
Prices on the site are estimates. Gold and silver prices follow the metal market, so Papa confirms the final price before a piece is sold or made.
Where is Afro Bijou Jewelry located?
Papa Gueye works in Miami, Florida, by appointment. There is no storefront. He serves customers across South Florida, from Miami-Dade and Broward up to Palm Beach County and down to the Keys. Call or text (305) 391-6937 to set a time.
