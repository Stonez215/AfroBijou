Gold jewelry, made by hand in 18k and 24k

Papa Gueye has worked gold for more than fifty years, in the Senegalese filigree tradition he learned at the anvil. Every design below can be made in 18k or 24k gold. Most are made to order in about 3 days, and Papa confirms the price against that day's gold market before he begins.

Prices are estimates. Gold and silver follow the metal market, and gold designs are available in 18k or 24k. Papa confirms the final price before any piece is sold or made.

Why Senegalese gold filigree is different

Filigree is gold pulled into wire finer than a hair, then twisted, coiled and soldered into open lace. Senegal is famous for it. Papa draws his own wire, so every leaf, scroll and rosette is shaped by hand rather than cast from a mold.

Gold for every occasion

Africa and Senegal map pendants, baobab and mask pendants, filigree crosses and hamsas, rosette earrings, wide cuffs and name sets. If you do not see what you want, Papa can make it.
