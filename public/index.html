Questions, answered

Where is Afro Bijou Jewelry located?

Papa Gueye works in Miami, Florida, by appointment. There is no storefront. He serves customers across South Florida, from Miami-Dade and Broward up to Palm Beach County and down to the Keys. Call or text (305) 391-6937 to set a time.

Who makes the jewelry?

Every piece is made by hand by Papa Gueye, a Senegalese goldsmith with more than fifty years at the bench who comes from a long ancestral line of gold and iron smiths. That includes the gold and silver work, the brass and copper pieces, and the beaded necklaces and bracelets.

Do you make custom gold jewelry?

Yes. Papa makes custom pieces in 18k and 24k gold and in sterling silver: name pieces, pendants, rings, cuffs, and remakes of family jewelry. A custom piece usually takes about 3 days. Larger or detailed work can take longer, and Papa tells you up front.

What metals and materials do you work in?

18k and 24k gold, 925 sterling silver, brass and copper, plus natural stones, beads and shells such as cowrie, tiger eye, onyx, jade, jasper, turquoise and lava stone.

How are prices set?

Prices on the site are estimates. Gold and silver prices follow the metal market, so Papa confirms the final price before a piece is sold or made.

Can I buy online?

Yes. Pieces marked In stock can be bought online and paid securely through Square. Made-to-order gold and silver designs are reserved online with a 50% deposit, and Papa calls you to confirm the final price before he starts.

Do you ship, or can I pick up?

Both. In-stock pieces ship for a $10 flat rate, or you can pick up free in Miami by appointment. Choose when you check out.
