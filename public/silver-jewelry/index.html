Silver jewelry with African character

Silver filigree bands, engraved saddle rings, and natural stones set in silver: black onyx, rose quartz, blue kyanite, turquoise, carnelian and smoky quartz. Many of Papa's gold designs can also be made in 925 sterling silver.
